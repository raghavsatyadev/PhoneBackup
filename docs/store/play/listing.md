## Full description (≤ 4000)

PhoneBackup sends the photos you choose from your phone straight to your own Windows PC, over your home Wi-Fi. There is no cloud, no account and no developer server: the photos go only to the computer you paired, and nowhere else.

SHARE TO YOUR PC
Pick photos in any gallery app, tap Share and choose PhoneBackup, or pick them inside the app. Give them an album and tags, check the dates, and send. Each photo shows its own progress, and a failed one can be retried. Big batches keep going in the background with a progress notification you can cancel.

KEEPS THE REAL DATE
PhoneBackup reads when each photo was taken (from the photo's EXIF data or its file name) and the PC keeps that date, so your albums stay in order. You can change the date of one photo or a whole group before sending.

BROWSE YOUR PC LIBRARY
The Library tab shows the photos on your PC by album, category and event, and can hide near-duplicate shots so you see only the best one. Swipe through them and pinch to zoom.

PAIR IN SECONDS
Keep the PhoneBackup PC app open, pick your PC from the list of computers on your Wi-Fi, and click Allow on the PC after checking the 6-digit code. You can pair more than one PC and choose which one receives photos.

NO PC YET?
Tap "Try with sample data" on the PC list to look around with a sample PC and sample photos. Nothing in sample mode is real, and nothing leaves your phone.

Needs the free PhoneBackup app on a Windows PC on the same Wi-Fi network: https://github.com/raghavsatyadev/PhoneBackup

## Instructions for reviewers (App access → "All or some functionality is restricted")

Name: Sample mode (no PC needed)

> The app sends photos to the user's own PC, which runs the PhoneBackup desktop app on the same
> Wi-Fi network. There is no account or login. To review without a PC: open the app. On the first
> run it opens the "PCs" list (later, tap the Settings icon at the top right of the Share tab). Scroll to the bottom
> and tap "Try with sample data". The app restarts, connected to a built-in sample PC, and shows a
> banner "Sample mode: a made-up PC with sample photos" at the top. Every screen works on the sample PC:
> - Share: pick photos, set an album and tags, and tap Send. Each photo's progress is shown, and the
>   background-transfer notification appears.
> - Library: browse sample photos by album, category and event, and open one to swipe and zoom.
> - Pairing: open the PCs list, tap the pencil next to "Sample PC", then tap "Pair". The app shows a
>   6-digit code, and the sample PC allows it after a moment.
>
> Nothing in sample mode leaves the phone. Tap "Exit" in the banner to leave sample mode.

No username or password is needed.
